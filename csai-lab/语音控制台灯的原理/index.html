把 32-bit 浮点权重压缩成 8-bit 整数。模型从几百 KB 缩小到几十 KB，精度几乎不损失。

Compress 32-bit float weights to 8-bit integers. Model shrinks from hundreds of KB to tens of KB, with almost no accuracy loss.

量化后的网络权重写入芯片 Flash。上电后：麦克风 → 特征提取 → 网络推理 → 控制灯——全在本地，一眨眼完成。

Quantised weights written to chip Flash. After power-on: mic → feature extraction → NN inference → control light — all local, over in a blink.

💡 市面上像启英泰伦 CI130X、NRK3303 等芯片内置专用神经网络处理器（BNPU / NPU），走的正是这条路线。85dB 强噪声下仍有 85% 识别率，响应 0.2–0.8 秒，功耗仅毫瓦级。💡 Real chips like CI130X and NRK3303 have a dedicated neural network processor (BNPU/NPU) inside — exactly this route. 85% recognition at 85dB noise, 0.2–0.8s response, milliwatt-class power.

?

两条路线谁更好？Which route is better?

没有绝对的"更好"。MFCC 路线成熟、可解释性强，仍在很多系统中使用。NN 路线在抗噪和泛化能力上往往更强，但需要训练数据和芯片支持。No absolute winner. MFCC is mature and interpretable, still widely used. NN often handles noise better and generalises more, but needs training data and chip support.

⚡

芯片上跑网络，耗电吗？Does NN on chip drain power?

关键词检测模型的推理功耗极低（毫瓦级）。一颗纽扣电池可以工作数月。硬件 NPU 专门优化了低精度的矩阵乘加运算。Keyword spotting inference uses milliwatts. A coin cell can last months. Hardware NPUs are specifically optimised for low-precision multiply-add.

🔒

为什么坚持离线？Why insist on offline?

离线意味着隐私、零延迟、不依赖 WiFi。台灯不需要听懂所有话——它只需要认出"开灯""关灯"这几个词。简单任务不需要云端大模型。Offline means privacy, zero latency, no WiFi dependence. A lamp doesn't need to understand everything — just recognise a few words. Simple tasks don't need cloud-scale models.

◎

MFCC 更容易保留什么？What does MFCC tend to keep?

平滑的频谱包络：能量大致在哪些频段隆起、又怎样随音节移动。它间接带有口腔 / 声道形状和发音动作的线索。The smooth spectral envelope: where energy rises across bands and how that shape moves across syllables. It carries indirect clues about vocal-tract shape and articulation.

⌇

它会弱化什么？What does it soften?

很细的谐波纹路、极端响度差和一些瞬时细节。Mel 合并邻近频率、log 压缩音量、DCT 只留低阶平滑形状——但绝不是全部删光。Very fine harmonic stripes, extreme loudness differences, and some tiny transients. Mel merges nearby frequencies, log compresses volume, and DCT keeps low-order smooth shape—but nothing is perfectly erased.

↻

口音与音色谁来“忽略”？Who learns to ignore accent and timbre?

不是 MFCC 单独做到的。板子还会做音量 / 均值归一化、降噪；最重要的是，模型要用不同说话人、口音和噪声训练，学会把无关差异当作背景。MFCC cannot do it alone. Boards may normalise loudness / average colour and reduce noise; most importantly, the model trains on many speakers, accents, and noisy rooms so it learns what is background.
